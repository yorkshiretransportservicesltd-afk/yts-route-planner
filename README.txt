YTS iPhone Home Screen App

Upload all files in this folder to a single HTTPS website, keeping the filenames and paths. Open the site in Safari on iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Data is stored locally in the browser. Back up existing data using Export Backup in your previous planner and Restore Backup in the hosted app. Opening a different URL will not automatically transfer your saved jobs.

Maps, routing, business lookup and other third-party services require internet access and are subject to their own availability and limits.
